# MediCheck AI 🩺🤖

MediCheck AI is an intelligent HealthTech solution designed to bridge the gap between patient symptoms and preliminary medical guidance. Powered by advanced AI models, it helps users analyze symptoms, offering fast, reliable, and explainable insights.

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Symptom Analysis:** Quick evaluate of common medical symptoms.
- **Explainable AI:** Provides clear context and reasoning behind recommendations.
- **Safety First:** Encourages proper medical consultation and safety disclaimers.

## 📝 Sample Inputs & Outputs

### Example 1: Common Cold / Symptoms
- **User Input:** "I have a mild fever, a runny nose, and a dry cough for 2 days. What should I do?"
- **MediCheck AI Output:** "Your symptoms align with a common viral cold. **Recommendation:** Rest, stay well-hydrated, and take over-the-counter pain relievers if needed. *Disclaimer: If your fever exceeds 38.5°C or lasts more than 3 days, please see a healthcare professional.*"

### Example 2: Medication Advice
- **User Input:** "Can I take Ibuprofen if I have a history of stomach ulcers?"
- **MediCheck AI Output:** "⚠️ **Caution:** NSAIDs like Ibuprofen can aggravate stomach ulcers and cause gastrointestinal distress. It is safer to use Acetaminophen/Paracetamol or consult your doctor for a suitable alternative."

### Example 3: General Health Guidance
- **User Input:** "I feel constant fatigue and headache after working 10 hours on a computer."
- **MediCheck AI Output:** "Your symptoms are likely linked to eye strain and lack of rest. **Advice:** Follow the 20-20-20 rule for screens, ensure at least 7-8 hours of sleep, and stay hydrated. Seek medical attention if headaches become severe or persistent."

## 🛠️ How to Run Locally
1. Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/Wafa424/MediCheck-AI.git`
2. Install dependencies: `pip install -r requirements.txt`
3. Run the application: `python app.py`
